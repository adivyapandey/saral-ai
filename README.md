# 🇮🇳 सरल AI (Saral AI) — AI Belongs to Everyone

> An open-source AI assistant translating complex bureaucratic regulations, legal fine prints, and academic rules into crystal-clear, 3-bullet Hindi summaries.

Built during **Hacktoberfest Hack Day Delhi NCR 2026** at Microsoft Office, Gurugram.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement
Millions of citizens and students encounter government notices, financial contracts, and academic compliance forms burdened with dense legal jargon. Language barriers and legalese prevent everyday people from understanding their fundamental rights and duties.

## 💡 Solution
**सरल AI** bridges this accessibility gap. It takes complex English legalese or bureaucratic documentation and distills it into actionable, plain-Hindi takeaways using open-source Large Language Models.
